## 1. The One-Paragraph Proof Statement

> *"I build leakage-free supervised ranking pipelines on noisy search engine telemetry that achieve 90.0% Precision@20 (+35 percentage points over heuristic baselines) under strict cross-client validation, built specifically for an **Applied Machine Learning Engineering Manager or Head of Search Science at a growth-stage data product company**, so that after reviewing my deployed research paper and reproducible holdout receipts, they **book a 20-minute technical interview call** to discuss hiring me for their ML engineering team."*

---

## 2. The One-Line Why

> *"A traditional CV or LinkedIn profile can list keywords like 'Scikit-Learn' and 'Gradient Boosting', but it cannot prove that my pipeline eliminates target leakage, generalizes across zero-overlap client holdouts, and translates statistical predictions into an operational human-in-the-loop action playbook without a live, reproducible research artifact."*

---

## 3. Structural Decomposition & Alignment Check

| Question Dimension | Defined Component | Alignment Verification |
|---|---|---|
| **The One Claim** | *Building leakage-free supervised ranking pipelines on tabular search telemetry.* | Single, named technical capability (not a vague list of multiple disconnected skills hiding behind "and"). |
| **The One Person** | *Applied ML Engineering Manager / Head of Search Science at a growth-stage data company.* | A specific hiring manager with the authority to initiate technical interviews, not generic "recruiters" or "people". |
| **The One Action** | *Book a 20-minute technical interview call.* | One primary, unambiguous, high-intent next step. |
| **Uniqueness & Specificity** | *Directly tied to the 30k-row Search Console portfolio, grouped client split, and +35 pp Precision@20 lift.* | Could only describe this specific research capstone and repository, not any generic portfolio. |
